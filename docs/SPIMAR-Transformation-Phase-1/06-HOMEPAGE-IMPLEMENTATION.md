# 06 — Homepage Implementation

## Homepage job

The homepage is an interactive B2B proposal. It must sell the decision to exhibit before presenting SPIMARIMMO as an institution.

## Chapter sequence

| # | Chapter | Required outcome |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Global header + exhibitor action | orient and expose the primary next action |
| 2 | Cinematic B2B hero | state audience, problem, promise, and action |
| 3 | Country/city event opportunities | make destinations, dates, states, and network tangible |
| 4 | Verified proof bar | establish sourced initial credibility |
| 5 | Why exhibit | connect benefits to proof or mechanism |
| 6 | Featured case or approved method fallback | reduce risk without invented outcomes |
| 7 | Before / during / after method | show the complete delivery system |
| 8 | 360-degree visibility | expose activities and one proof artifact per promise |
| 9 | MRE demand intelligence | explain market depth with dated sources |
| 10 | Trusted ecosystem | show only authorized valid relationships |
| 11 | Video testimonials | use approved identities, roles, captions, consent, poster |
| 12 | Real event gallery | documentary atmosphere, attendance, meetings, stands |
| 13 | Offers comparison | compare approved capabilities and availability honestly |
| 14 | Resources | brochure, guide, calendar, plans, checklist |
| 15 | Exhibitor FAQ | resolve commercial objections |
| 16 | Insights | connect expertise to events/destinations/offers |
| 17 | About/team/media confidence | identify the organization and owners |
| 18 | Qualified final conversion | choose brochure, enquiry, proposal, or meeting |
| 19 | Complete footer | navigation, contacts, legal, preferences, recovery |

Country/city event opportunities must remain within the first three major content chapters.

## Hero contract

- authentic, authorized event video or an approved editorial poster;
- never autoplay with sound;
- mobile and constrained-network poster path;
- no layout shift or unreadable text over media;
- B2B promise, supporting evidence cue, primary exhibitor CTA, secondary brochure CTA;
- trust signal visible before the first scroll when content is approved;
- reduced-motion behavior without loss of context.

## Content resilience

If proof, media, case, offer, or event data is not approved:

- hide the claim;
- show a transparent readiness state where useful;
- use a designed typographic/media fallback;
- use an approved method/resource alternative;
- never publish generated filler as documentary evidence.

## Responsive behavior

Mobile becomes one clear decision path:

```text
Promise -> next event -> proof -> method -> offer/action
```

- reduce simultaneous motion and media load;
- keep primary CTA reachable without obstructing content;
- preserve source labels and status semantics;
- move, collapse, or sequence modules intentionally;
- verify Arabic hierarchy and logical alignment independently.

## Analytics

At minimum:

- hero CTA selected;
- event opportunity selected;
- proof/case/source opened;
- offer/resource selected;
- form started, validation error, durable submission;
- locale changed;
- media fallback used;
- integration-delayed confirmation shown.

No PII enters analytics.

## Acceptance

- 90-second comprehension test passes with target decision-makers;
- events appear early and have accurate status;
- every visible sensitive claim is governed;
- all 19 chapters have approved present/alternate/hidden rules;
- desktop/mobile/RTL/reduced-motion/keyboard states pass;
- LCP, INP, CLS, JS, and media budgets pass;
- no House of Yellow brand/content residue remains.
