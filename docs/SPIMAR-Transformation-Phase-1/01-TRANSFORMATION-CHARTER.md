# 01 — Transformation Charter

## Mission

Convert the reusable Next.js reference foundation into one coherent SPIMARIMMO platform that:

- reduces the decision risk of exhibitors;
- makes the international event network concrete early;
- proves the before/during/after delivery model;
- creates qualified and attributable commercial opportunities;
- supports a separate, efficient visitor path;
- gives content and commercial teams governed operational tools.

## Controlling audiences

| Priority | Audience | Decision job | Primary proof needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | General management / property developer leadership | Validate credibility, risk, timing, and strategic value | verified reach, method, governance, commercial fit |
| 1 | Commercial leadership | Evaluate lead quality, qualification, ownership, and follow-up | acquisition logic, appointment process, lead handoff, case evidence |
| 1 | Marketing leadership | Evaluate visibility, content, media, attribution, and reporting | campaign artifacts, deliverables, source-backed metrics |
| 2 | MRE / investor visitor | Find a relevant event, understand it, and preregister | date, place, programme, exhibitors, practical information |
| Internal | Content, commercial, operations, management | Publish truthful content and operate leads/events | roles, workflows, audit, state control, recovery |

The corporate homepage prioritizes exhibitor decision-making. An event or campaign page may prioritize visitors when its context requires it without erasing exhibitor context.

## Product principles

1. Value before form.
2. Evidence before promise.
3. Events within the first three major homepage chapters.
4. Exhibitor and visitor actions remain distinct in content, forms, states, and analytics.
5. Unknown data is never invented.
6. Real event proof uses authorized documentary media.
7. Durable submission is separate from CRM sync, email delivery, booking, and business outcome.
8. Mobile and Arabic RTL are intentional compositions, not reduced desktop copies.
9. CMS and CRM controls perform real authorized operations or are visibly unavailable.
10. The public name is `SPIMARIMMO` until the brand owner approves another form.

## Source authority

| Concern | Controlling authority |
|---|---|
| Business objective, audience, evidence, and conversion | Official SPIMARIMMO strategic/UX/content specification and approved later owner decisions |
| Product/UX/design contracts | Approved PRD, route inventory, 48 UX/HIF targets, 144 states, identity, and design-system work |
| Reference engineering craft | Frozen House of Yellow foundation and retained QA evidence |
| Current implementation state | Repository, Git history, executed tests, deployment, and runtime evidence |
| CMS/CRM production choice | Approved architecture decision record after provider audit/POC |
| Earlier generated screens | Reference only; never deterministic implementation truth |

When sources conflict, classify the conflict, apply the authority for that concern, and record the resolution. Never silently select the easier requirement.

| CMS | authenticated roles, CRUD, translation, preview, review, publish, revision, archive, audit, revalidation |
| CRM | durable submission, deduplication, consent, attribution, assignment, pipeline, activities, tasks, appointment and retry states |
| Quality | functional, visual, responsive, RTL, accessibility, performance, privacy, security, SEO, analytics, and operational tests |

## Success measures

- A B2B decision-maker can explain the offer, destinations, proof, method, and next action within 90 seconds.
- Every public claim has a source, period, scope, owner, approval state, and expiry/withdrawal behavior where applicable.
- Every public conversion has a durable record and an honest confirmation state.
- Every published route has locale, indexation, canonical, structured-data, accessibility, responsive, and content-readiness decisions.
- No House of Yellow production residue remains.
