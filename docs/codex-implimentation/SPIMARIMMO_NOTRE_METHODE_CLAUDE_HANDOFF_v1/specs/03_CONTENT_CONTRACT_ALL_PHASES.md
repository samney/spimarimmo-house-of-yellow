## Footer journey

The three labels remain visible across all phases:

1. `01 PRÉPARER`
2. `02 ACTIVER`
3. `03 TRANSFORMER`

The active phase changes; completed phases receive a check state.

## Content prohibitions

Do not introduce:

- invented dates or venue names;
- fake numbers or performance values;
- personal lead data;
- invented client logos;
- unapproved testimonials;
- prices or package claims;
- city names that are not supplied by the CMS;
- filler text inside visible documents.

Use neutral layout bars for document microcopy that has not been approved.
