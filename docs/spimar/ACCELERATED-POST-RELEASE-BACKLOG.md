Legal text must be authored and approved. `LEG-1` stays open.                                                                                                                                                       |

Substituting Supabase is a change to `lib/spimar/repository.ts` and `lib/spimar/auth.ts` only.                                                                                                                             |
| **Email / calendar / external CRM (`P-2`)** | No providers connected. Leads are stored and worked internally; nothing is sent to a contact, and no surface claims otherwise.                                                                                                                                                                         |
