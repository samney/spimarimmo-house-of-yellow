## Phase decision

Three composition territories were evaluated at Gate 6. They did not propose different palettes:

1. `IDT-01 — Global Exhibition Signal`
2. `IDT-02 — Morocco Across Cities`
3. `IDT-03 — Proof of Presence`

The approved direction is a governed blend:

> `IDT-01` controls the core brand and campaign system; `IDT-02` contributes warmth, belonging, and city-network storytelling; `IDT-03` is reserved for proof, method, reporting, and offer-comparison modules.

This is not a decorative average. Each territory has a defined job and cannot introduce a competing primary identity or campaign color.

The brand palette decision is already recorded:

> Use the existing SPIMARIMMO black and gold identity from the current public site. The live digital gold is `#EFC337`; black is `#000000`. White and gray are supporting neutrals only. Other chromatic colors are limited to bounded semantic feedback and never become campaign or audience themes.

## Boundaries carried forward

- SPIMAR Gold `#EFC337` and Black `#000000` are the fixed digital brand direction, verified from the current public site and confirmed by the owner.
- Source brand masters remain required for logo geometry, print conversions, spot-color definitions, and final production governance.
- Existing logo geometry is not reconstructed from screenshots. A provisional text lockup is used in working applications.
- Generated or unverified imagery may illustrate mood internally but cannot be published as historical evidence.
- House of Yellow contributes a desired craft bar only; its measurements, components, brand, copy, and media are not imported.
- The 48 `UXF` targets and 144 states remain the structural contract.
- Phase 07 may tokenize only the Gate 6-approved direction.
