# SPIMARIMMO Art-Direction Territories

**Document ID:** `SPM-IDT-001`  
**Status:** `IDT_01A_SELECTED_AT_GATE_6`  
**Date:** 31 July 2026

## 1. Evaluation method

Each territory is evaluated against the same controlling criteria:

| Criterion | Question |
|---|---|
| Event clarity | Is a real edition, city, date, and action unmistakable? |
| Property relevance | Does the experience visibly concern Moroccan property projects and decisions? |
| Commercial credibility | Can a developer decision unit see method, proof, fit, and next commitment? |
| Moroccan distinctiveness | Does the brand feel contemporary and connected to Morocco without cliché? |
| Multi-city scalability | Can one parent brand support Paris, Brussels, Montréal, Abu Dhabi, and future editions? |
| Multilingual strength | Does it remain coherent in French, English, and Arabic RTL? |
| Content resilience | Does it still work when media, metrics, partners, or registration are unavailable? |
| Craft potential | Can it reach the desired editorial, motion, responsive, and media quality bar? |

The product structure and brand palette remain constant. Only presentation hierarchy, typographic voice, imagery direction, graphic language, and motion character vary.

### Fixed palette invariant

Every territory uses the established public identity:

- SPIMAR Gold `#EFC337`;
- Black `#000000`;
- White and gray as supporting neutrals;
- no blue, green, terracotta, or other competing campaign palette;
- non-brand hues only for bounded semantic feedback where wording and shape also communicate meaning.

## 2. `IDT-01` — Global Exhibition Signal

### Concept

SPIMARIMMO behaves like an authoritative international event signal: immediate, bold, physical, and easy to orient. City and date become graphic anchors; real event media supplies energy; SPIMAR Gold directs attention and action.

### Visual character

- high-contrast black/white rhythm;
- oversized condensed city and edition typography;
- gold signal blocks, rules, and active-state markers;
- hard editorial crops with visible event scale;
- compact technical metadata beside expressive display type;
- asymmetric grids with strong alignment rather than floating cards;
- dark media stages balanced by readable light decision sections.

### Fixed palette application

| Role | Candidate |
|---|---|
| Signal | SPIMAR Gold `#EFC337` |
| Black | `#000000` |
| Graphite support | `#444444` |
| White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Mid gray | `#878787` |
| Light gray/rule | `#CCCCCC` |

### Typography

- Latin display: condensed grotesk with poster authority;
- Latin body/UI: neutral, highly legible grotesk;
- Arabic display/body: contemporary Arabic sans with matching density and clear numerals;
- city, date, and edition may become large typographic marks, but factual labels remain semantic text.

### Imagery

- crowded exhibition wide shots;
- serious table/model conversations;
- developer booths and project displays;
- clear host-city and Moroccan project context;
- portrait crops showing decision and guidance, not generic celebration.

### Motion

- decisive image-plane reveals;
- section labels and city/date transitions;
- staggered event rail with explicit controls;
- static first meaning before animation;
- poster fallback for every video-led composition.

### Strengths

- fastest five-second comprehension;
- strongest exhibitor-first commercial presence;
- compatible with the existing black/gold recognition;
- scales naturally into campaigns and editions;
- closest to the desired high-craft implementation foundation without copying it.

### Risks

- can become generic event-tech if property and Morocco imagery are weak;
- can feel too aggressive if every section uses black/gold at maximum contrast;
- condensed typography requires careful Arabic recomposition.

### Best-fit surfaces

`UXF-001`, `UXF-003`, `UXF-004`, `UXF-013`, `UXF-016`, `UXF-046`

## 3. `IDT-02` — Morocco Across Cities

### Concept

SPIMARIMMO makes the relationship between Morocco and international host cities visible. The experience feels warmer, more human, and more place-driven while retaining event discipline and exhibitor purpose.

### Visual character

- white/light-neutral editorial canvas with black structure and controlled gold signals;
- city-to-city route logic expressed through lines, coordinates, edition indexes, and image sequencing;
- paired photography: Moroccan projects/places with real host-city event moments;
- generous editorial fields interrupted by strong date/action modules;
- tactile material cues from contemporary Moroccan architecture, never ornamental pattern overlays.

### Fixed palette application

| Role | Candidate |
|---|---|
| Signal | SPIMAR Gold `#EFC337` |
| Black | `#000000` |
| Graphite support | `#444444` |
| White/light editorial field | `#FFFFFF` |
| Mid gray | `#878787` |
| Light gray/rule | `#CCCCCC` |

### Typography

- wide contemporary grotesk for narrative headlines;
- compact sans for dates, routes, and metadata;
- Arabic compositions gain intentional vertical rhythm and do not mimic Latin line breaks;
- place names can carry the identity without inventing city logos.

### Imagery

- real Moroccan residential developments and urban context;
- visitors and advisers in meaningful interactions;
- restrained host-city context;
- architectural details as transitions, not decorative background;
- paired “origin/opportunity/encounter” image sequences.

### Motion

- route progression between city editions;
- restrained cross-crop image transitions;
- map/edition change shown through labels and position, not cinematic zoom;
- no continuous decorative route animation.

### Strengths

- most distinctively Moroccan and emotionally relevant;
- strong for the MRE visitor journey and city-network storytelling;
- creates editorial warmth missing from all-dark explorations;
- supports longer content and institutional trust.

### Risks

- can drift into tourism or residential lifestyle marketing;
- the exhibitor value proposition may weaken if event mechanics and proof appear late;
- warmth must come from real people, place, material, crops, and typography rather than an added color theme.

### Best-fit surfaces

`UXF-013`, `UXF-014`, `UXF-016`, `UXF-025`, `UXF-035`, `UXF-040`

## 4. `IDT-03` — Proof of Presence

### Concept

SPIMARIMMO is presented as an evidence-led operating partner. The system makes method, expected deliverables, event states, campaign outputs, and sourced outcomes exceptionally clear.

### Visual character

- disciplined editorial/data grid;
- strong black and white contrast with gold as selection/action;
- source labels, periods, definitions, and caveats integrated into composition;
- documentary media paired with structured evidence, not isolated decorative dashboards;
- modular rows and comparison planes instead of card walls;
- less expressive hero treatment; greater depth in proof, offers, and resources.

### Fixed palette application

| Role | Candidate |
|---|---|
| Signal | SPIMAR Gold `#EFC337` |
| Black | `#000000` |
| Graphite support | `#444444` |
| White evidence field | `#FFFFFF` |
| Mid gray metadata | `#878787` |
| Light gray/rule | `#CCCCCC` |

### Typography

- neutral grotesk with clear numerals and tabular figures;
- condensed display reserved for chapter and edition markers;
- metadata hierarchy remains readable at small sizes;
- Arabic tables and definitions use logical alignment, localized dates, and isolated mixed-direction values.

### Imagery

- evidence-bearing images: real deliverables, campaign artifacts, event environments, meetings, reports;
- captions always identify edition and context;
- projects shown beside the commercial or visitor question they answer;
- no abstract data decoration without a real sourced measure.

### Motion

- state transitions and evidence reveals;
- comparison changes animate only when meaning remains obvious;
- metric animation only for verified values;
- restrained chapter progression rather than cinematic page movement.

### Strengths

- strongest for executive trust, offers, method, cases, and resources;
- naturally reinforces product truth and operational states;
- resilient when large campaign media is unavailable;
- clear foundation for a rigorous design system.

### Risks

- can look like a SaaS dashboard if over-modularized;
- weakest emotional/event signal as a whole-site master direction;
- can undervalue live atmosphere and Moroccan belonging.

### Best-fit surfaces

`UXF-006`, `UXF-008`, `UXF-011`, `UXF-012`, `UXF-035`, `UXF-047`

## 6. Recommended identity architecture

### Recommendation: `IDT-01A — Signal with Moroccan editorial depth`

Use:

- `IDT-01` as the controlling brand, hero, navigation, edition, campaign, and conversion language;
- `IDT-02` for destination storytelling, visitor orientation, project/place media, and human warmth;
- `IDT-03` for proof, method, case, offer, comparison, resource, and reporting modules.

The system keeps one permanent identity:

- one parent wordmark;
- one primary SPIMAR Gold signal;
- one black/white/gray neutral foundation;
- one bilingual typographic system;
- one event/city/date grammar;
- one media-authenticity policy.

Territory-specific ideas are composition modes, not separate themes. Users should not feel that they entered three different websites.
