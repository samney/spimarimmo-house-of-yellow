# SPIMARIMMO Brand Strategy and Positioning Translation

**Document ID:** `SPM-BRAND-001`  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** `APPROVED_AT_GATE_6`  
**Date:** 31 July 2026

## 1. Brand role

SPIMARIMMO is the international exhibition platform that helps Moroccan property developers present credible projects to Moroccans abroad and helps visitors evaluate property opportunities through direct, organized access to developers, financing partners, and guidance.

The brand is neither a luxury property broker nor a generic conference organizer. It must behave as:

- an event authority;
- a commercial access partner for exhibitors;
- a trusted orientation layer for visitors;
- a multi-city Moroccan property network;
- an evidence-led operator before, during, and after each edition.

## 2. Core positioning

### 2.1 Category

`International Moroccan property exhibition and commercial-access platform`

### 2.2 Primary audience

Moroccan property developers and their general, commercial, and marketing decision units.

### 2.3 Secondary audience

Moroccans abroad and international investors seeking relevant projects, financing access, and direct in-person guidance.

### 2.4 Strategic promise

> SPIMARIMMO turns an international exhibition presence into a structured market-access programme with visible preparation, qualified encounters, documented activation, and accountable follow-up.

The promise is a positioning statement, not a public performance claim. Actual outcomes require approved definitions, sources, dates, and caveats.

### 2.5 Reason to believe

The interface should demonstrate the reason to believe through approved evidence:

- real editions and event records;
- recognizable organizers, promoters, banks, institutions, and media where permissions exist;
- a clear before/during/after method;
- documented campaign and visibility deliverables;
- case studies with sourced expected and actual outcomes;
- real documentary media and participant testimony;
- transparent event, offer, registration, and availability states.

## 3. Brand idea

### Working brand idea

> **Moroccan property, present in the markets that matter.**

This idea connects four required signals:

| Signal | Expression |
|---|---|
| Event | A real date, place, edition, programme, and physical gathering |
| Property | Projects, destinations, financing, and serious decision-making |
| International | A visible network of host cities and market-specific editions |
| Morocco | Contemporary belonging and opportunity without heritage decoration or tourism clichés |

## 4. Brand personality

| Attribute | Must feel like | Must not become |
|---|---|---|
| Confident | Clear decisions, large hierarchy, decisive actions | Loud hype or unsupported superlatives |
| International | Multi-city, multilingual, operationally consistent | Generic airport/corporate imagery |
| Energetic | Live event atmosphere and purposeful movement | Festival graphics or constant animation |
| Credible | Sources, owners, states, evidence, real people | Logo quantity used as a substitute for outcomes |
| Contemporary Moroccan | Modern architecture, people, markets, material and place | Ornament, flags, zellige, or green/red clichés as decoration |
| Commercial | Explicit exhibitor value and progressive commitment | Aggressive sales language or fake urgency |
| Human | Serious conversations, advisers, developers, visitors | Stock handshakes and anonymous crowds |

## 5. Audience-specific expression

| Audience | Lead message | Visual emphasis | Preferred action |
|---|---|---|---|
| General management | Strategic fit, legitimacy, downside clarity | Scale, track record, governance, sourced cases | Senior meeting or qualified request |
| Commercial leadership | Prospect quality, access, follow-up | Encounter mechanics, qualification, case outcomes | Event-specific proposal |
| Marketing leadership | Reach, activation, reusable visibility | Campaign artifacts, media, reporting, content outputs | Compare offers or request activation detail |
| Returning exhibitor | What changed and where to go next | Edition continuity, new markets, prior context | Re-engage on a specific edition |
| Visitor/MRE investor | What is available, where, and how to prepare | Projects, people, venue, financing, practical access | Short event pre-registration |
| Partner/media | Legitimacy and approved materials | Organizers, resources, press assets, routed contacts | Resource or routed enquiry |

## 6. Verbal identity

### 6.1 Voice principles

- Lead with the decision or benefit, then explain the mechanism.
- Name the market, edition, audience, and real action explicitly.
- Prefer concrete nouns and verbs over abstract institutional language.
- Distinguish expectation, capability, delivery, and measured outcome.
- Use evidence labels, sources, dates, definitions, and caveats near claims.
- Keep CTAs truthful: `Demander une proposition`, not `Réserver un stand`, until a transaction really exists.
- Use confident sentences without false certainty.

## 7. Brand architecture

| Layer | Role | Naming behavior | Visual behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parent brand | Global B2B authority and network | `SPIMARIMMO` | Owns the permanent SPIMAR Gold `#EFC337` and Black `#000000` identity and core typography |
| Edition | One host city/market and event lifecycle | `SPIMARIMMO — Paris`, for example | City/date becomes the primary campaign identifier; no new independent logo required by default |
| Programme/path | Exhibitor, visitor, resources, proof | Descriptive labels | Uses parent identity; audience differences come from hierarchy and content, not competing colors |
| Partner activation | Approved co-branded placement | Controlled lockup | Must preserve clear ownership and legal logo permissions |
| Campaign | Time-bounded edition communication | Edition, city, date, theme if approved | Uses the parent black/gold identity; distinction comes from media, city/date typography, composition, and content—not a competing campaign color |

## 8. Five-second identity test

A representative first view should communicate, without reading the full page:

1. this is a real international event platform;
2. it concerns Moroccan property;
3. a specific city/edition is active or relevant;
4. credible people, projects, or evidence exist;
5. the next exhibitor or visitor action is explicit.

Any visual direction that fails two or more signals is rejected regardless of aesthetic quality.

## 9. Identity invariants

| ID | Invariant |
|---|---|
| `BRI-001` | SPIMAR Gold is the existing warm digital gold `#EFC337`; it is never rendered as a metallic gradient, foil effect, or generic luxury-gold treatment. |
| `BRI-002` | Real event and property media lead over abstract decorative geometry. |
| `BRI-003` | City/date/edition identity is visible before decorative campaign language. |
| `BRI-004` | Evidence modules remain visually adjacent to the claim or mechanism they support. |
| `BRI-005` | Exhibitor and visitor actions are differentiated by wording and hierarchy, not by misleading success/availability colors. |
| `BRI-006` | Arabic, French, and English remain one brand with script-aware composition. |
| `BRI-007` | The identity cannot imply ticketing, inventory reservation, guaranteed outcomes, or approved partners when those facts are absent. |
| `BRI-008` | House of Yellow craft can influence quality, but no reference brand/content is reproduced. |
| `BRI-009` | The parent and every edition are recognizable without inventing a new logo for each city. |
| `BRI-010` | Reduced-motion and media-failure states preserve the intended composition and meaning. |
| `BRI-011` | The permanent chromatic brand palette is SPIMAR Gold and Black; white and gray are supporting neutrals, while other hues are restricted to bounded semantic feedback. |

## 10. Rejection criteria

Reject a direction that:

- resembles a luxury developer brochure more than an event platform;
- could belong to a generic SaaS, finance dashboard, or agency;
- relies on turquoise/green as the primary brand identity;
- introduces blue, terracotta, green, or another campaign hue as a competing brand identity;
- hides the edition, city, date, or audience path;
- uses generated event imagery as proof;
- depends on pills, glass cards, gradients, or abstract arches for character;
- becomes empty and under-art-directed on desktop;
- treats mobile or Arabic as a scaled or mirrored afterthought;
- uses partner logos, figures, dates, prices, or testimonials without approval.
