## 4. Lessons from the visual waves

### Keep

- recognizable black and SPIMAR Gold contrast;
- large contemporary typographic moments;
- documentary event media and visible human interaction;
- edition/city/date as graphic identity;
- light/dark editorial rhythm;
- country/event cards as a signature system;
- proof, offers, method, and gallery shown as different compositions;
- dense desktop craft with intentionally recomposed mobile layouts;
- the latest wave’s strong event scale and commercial confidence.

### Refine

- gold usage must become governed and accessible rather than applied everywhere;
- dark environments should anchor media/proof, not reduce all long-form readability;
- city and property imagery needs a controlled crop and rights model;
- typography needs one coherent bilingual system;
- proof panels need verified definitions and sources, not placeholder metrics;
- content density needs clear scanning hierarchy rather than repeated dashboard panels.

### Reject

- turquoise/green as the primary brand identity;
- blue, terracotta, or any other chromatic campaign theme competing with black/gold;
- generic luxury real-estate styling;
- abstract arches and decorative Moroccan motifs as the main story;
- endless rounded cards, pills, floating glass, gradients, and soft SaaS dashboards;
- heavy dashboard language on public marketing pages;
- invented crowds, figures, partners, testimonials, dates, venues, or prices;
- empty desktop layouts that rely on scale alone;
- mobile treated as a shrunk desktop page.

## 5. Logo policy for Phase 06

Until vector masters are supplied:

- use `SPIMARIMMO` as a plain provisional text lockup in working applications;
- do not redraw, trace, stretch, or “improve” the observed raster logo;
- do not invent an Arabic logo;
- preserve a neutral clear area around the provisional lockup;
- avoid placing the mark on uncontrolled photography without a tested contrast treatment;
- do not create edition sub-logos unless the approved brand architecture later requires them.

Gate 6 selects art direction, not final logo geometry.

## 6. Media authenticity and rights

Every production media item requires:

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Identity | Stable asset ID and canonical filename |
| Context | Edition, date, city, venue, project, and people shown |
| Ownership | Creator/licensor and source file |
| Permission | Web, advertising, social, print, partner, duration, geography |
| People | Release/consent status where identifiable |
| Brand marks | Permission for logos and signage shown |
| Editorial truth | Caption and whether the media is historical, current, illustrative, or planned |
| Accessibility | Alt text, transcript/captions, poster, and focal point |
| Lifecycle | Expiry, revocation, replacement, and archive behavior |

Generated imagery may be used for internal mood exploration only and must be labeled as conceptual. It cannot appear in a proof, testimonial, partner, historical-edition, or quantified-outcome context.

## 7. Required source package

### P0 before final identity approval

- primary vector logo and all official variants;
- confirmation: retain, refine, or redesign the current mark;
- official source/print color definitions; digital anchors are `#EFC337` and `#000000` unless the brand owner supplies a newer approved master;
- approved Arabic brand-name treatment;
- font files/licensing constraints if an existing corporate family must be kept;
- representative rights-cleared event and property media;
- list of launch locales and numeral/date preferences;
- brand owner and final approval authority.

### P1 before high-fidelity full-site approval

- partner/developer/bank logo package with permissions;
- approved cases, testimonials, metrics, and reporting samples;
- edition campaign assets and event records;
- legal rules for sponsorship/co-branding;
- photo/video capture brief for missing content categories;
- social, email, print, signage, and presentation application priorities.

## 8. Current audit conclusion

There is enough evidence to select an art-direction system and lock the digital black/gold direction, but not enough controlled source material to finalize the logo, publish factual imagery, define print colors, or lock every derived production token.

The appropriate Gate 6 decision is therefore:

> approve a visual territory over the confirmed digital black/gold identity, while keeping source-logo geometry, print/spot color masters, derived neutral scales, typography licensing, production media rights, and co-branding rules as explicit owned inputs to Phase 07.
