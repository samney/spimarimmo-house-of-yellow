# Phase 09 — Prototype, Motion and Presentation Mockups

**Phase:** 09  
**Status:** `COMPLETE_READY_FOR_GATE_9`  
**Date:** 31 July 2026  
**Controlling inputs:** approved PRD, sitemap, journeys, 48 `UXF` targets, 48 `HIF` targets, 144-state contract, `IDT-01A`, Phase 07 design system, Phase 08 clone-convergence strategy

## Purpose

Phase 09 turns the approved SPIMAR experience into a reviewable interaction and presentation layer. It verifies representative critical journeys, motion behavior, truthful conversion outcomes, responsive/RTL transformations, exception recovery, and stakeholder presentation scenes before technical architecture and implementation.

This phase does not modify the House of Yellow clone and does not claim production behavior, real provider integration, final content, or user validation.

## What Phase 09 proves

- critical decision and conversion paths remain coherent after high-fidelity treatment;
- durable submission, provider sync/delivery, meeting booking, and business qualification remain distinct;
- event lifecycle, exhibitor-sales availability, and visitor-registration availability remain independent;
- navigation, actions, forms, confirmations, closed states, provider failures, locale fallback, mobile, RTL, and reduced motion have explicit behavior;
- motion supports orientation and feedback without delaying meaning;
- the approved SPIMAR experience can later converge onto the repaired clone without becoming a clean-slate production implementation.

## What remains controlled

- fixture copy, dates, media, cases, partners, prices, metrics, and provider responses are not production facts;
- final typefaces, vector logo geometry, Arabic lockup, rights-cleared media, and production integrations remain owned inputs;
- moderated validation remains required before any user-validation claim;
- browser, assistive-technology, performance, and real-provider evidence remain implementation work;
- House of Yellow repository identity and parity remain blockers for code convergence.

## Gate rule

Gate 9 may approve the prototype and motion contract as the interaction specification for Phase 10. It must not authorize clone adaptation until the reference foundation is identified, repaired, and accepted.
