## Phase result

The approved Product Foundation has been converted into a Release 1 contract covering:

- product boundary, goals, audiences, and roles;
- global/event/exhibitor/visitor experiences;
- offers, proof, resources, and editorial behavior;
- conversion forms, visitor registration, CRM handoff, and failure recovery;
- structured CMS and content governance;
- host/subdomain and FR/EN/AR/RTL behavior;
- analytics, SEO, accessibility, performance, security, privacy, and operations;
- release acceptance, dependencies, risks, and owned open decisions;
- complete traceability to all 40 requirements extracted from the CTO source.

The PRD intentionally does not select a final CMS/CRM provider or invent event facts, prices, proof, legal policy, or operational SLA.
