# Spimar Immo — Market Research and Inspiration System

**Project:** Spimar Immo Digital Experience Redesign  
**Prepared for:** Clarkom pre-onboarding assignment  
**Research date:** 29 July 2026  
**Status:** Research baseline for UX architecture and creative direction  

---

## 1. Research objective

This study identifies current market references that can help Spimar evolve from a collection of old, disconnected campaign pages into a premium, multilingual event-conversion platform.

The research is not intended to produce a visual clone. Its purpose is to identify:

- proven event-registration patterns;
- effective real-estate positioning;
- premium but usable visual systems;
- multi-location event architecture;
- trust and social-proof patterns;
- exhibitor and partner discovery;
- multilingual and RTL requirements;
- suitable photography, motion and video directions;
- patterns that can scale through a CMS;
- design mistakes Spimar should avoid.

---

## 2. Spimar's relevant market category

Spimar sits at the intersection of four categories:

1. International real-estate exhibitions
2. Diaspora investment and home-ownership events
3. Moroccan property and lifestyle marketing
4. Multi-location event and lead-generation platforms

Its competitive experience should therefore not resemble only a property portal or only a conference website.

The desired experience is:

> A trusted international gateway where Moroccans abroad can meet verified property developers, banks and advisers, compare opportunities, and prepare a property decision in Morocco.

---

## 3. Executive findings

### 3.1 What the best platforms currently do

The strongest event websites:

- show the city, date, venue and main CTA immediately;
- lead with outcomes instead of a generic event description;
- use real attendance, exhibitor and event photography as proof;
- quantify credibility early;
- distinguish visitor, exhibitor and partner journeys;
- make exhibitor discovery a major conversion asset;
- explain the agenda as a sequence of useful outcomes;
- keep registration visible throughout the page;
- archive earlier editions to create institutional credibility;
- organize multiple locations inside one consistent platform;
- adapt messaging and imagery by market without redesigning every page.

### 3.2 Where Spimar can differentiate

Most international property exhibitions communicate scale, investment and networking. Spimar can own a more focused emotional and practical position:

> Your trusted route from life abroad to a real property opportunity in Morocco.

This creates a differentiated combination of:

- Moroccan belonging;
- verified opportunities;
- face-to-face access;
- financing guidance;
- reduced uncertainty;
- one-place comparison;
- international convenience.

### 3.3 Recommended experience formula

Spimar should combine:

- **Cityscape:** international event-platform structure;
- **SIMA:** numerical proof and ecosystem clarity;
- **Dreamforce:** benefit-led storytelling and agenda framing;
- **SMAP Immo:** diaspora-specific category relevance;
- **Amali Properties:** premium architectural art direction;
- **Canada International Property Show:** practical visitor motivations;
- **United Property Expo:** multi-city calendar logic.

No single reference solves the complete Spimar use case.

## 5. Pattern research by UX area

### 5.1 Hero

**Market pattern**

- event identity;
- host city;
- exact date;
- venue;
- concise visitor outcome;
- primary registration action;
- real event or architectural imagery;
- visible organizer or partner credibility.

**Spimar direction**

The global homepage hero should introduce the platform and surface the next relevant events. A location page hero should immediately become event-specific.

Recommended event hero content:

- Eyebrow: `The Moroccan Real Estate Event in Paris`
- Headline: `Meet Morocco's trusted property developers—without leaving France`
- Supporting statement: compare projects, meet banks and receive guidance;
- Date and venue;
- `Reserve my free invitation`;
- `Explore exhibitors`;
- short credibility indicator;
- real prior-event photography or an honest campaign visual.

**Tags**

`event hero`, `split hero`, `editorial hero`, `architectural photography`, `city and date`, `sticky register CTA`, `full-bleed image`, `premium dark landing page`

### 5.2 Trust and evidence

**Market pattern**

SIMA and Cityscape use quantified scale; MIPIM uses institutional and media authority; high-converting event pages add speaker, exhibitor or previous-edition proof.

**Spimar direction**

Trust should be layered:

1. Verified numbers
2. Recognizable developers and banks
3. Real event photography
4. Previous cities and editions
5. Testimonials with identity and context
6. Organizer and data-use transparency

Never use unverified counters or generated imagery as documentary proof.

**Tags**

`event statistics`, `logo wall`, `social proof strip`, `previous edition`, `press coverage`, `attendee testimonial`, `verified exhibitors`

### 5.3 Benefits

**Market pattern**

Dreamforce and Cityscape describe what the attendee gains rather than merely listing event features.

**Spimar direction**

Organize visitor benefits around decisions:

- Discover verified projects
- Compare cities and developers
- Discuss financing
- Ask legal and practical questions
- Receive personalized orientation
- Continue with qualified contacts after the event

**Tags**

`outcome-led copy`, `benefit cards`, `decision journey`, `visitor value`, `why attend`

### 5.4 Exhibitors and partners

**Market pattern**

Strong property events treat exhibitors as a searchable reason to attend, not a decorative logo carousel.

**Spimar direction**

Create categorized cards:

- Property developers
- Banks and financing
- Institutional partners
- Property services
- Organizer

Each card may contain:

- logo;
- category;
- Moroccan cities covered;
- project types;
- short description;
- event participation status.

**Tags**

`exhibitor directory`, `partner grid`, `category filters`, `logo taxonomy`, `developer cards`, `bank partners`

### 5.5 Agenda and event utility

**Market pattern**

Major conferences frame the agenda by what happens and what the visitor should prepare.

**Spimar direction**

The agenda should remain easy for a consumer visitor:

- opening hours;
- expert talks;
- financing consultations;
- project presentations;
- venue map;
- transport;
- accessibility;
- what to bring;
- add to calendar.

**Tags**

`agenda timeline`, `day tabs`, `event utility`, `venue map`, `add to calendar`, `visitor checklist`

### 5.6 Registration

**Market pattern**

Conversion-focused pages repeat one consistent registration CTA and reduce initial form friction.

**Spimar direction**

Use a short first step:

- full name;
- email;
- WhatsApp/phone;
- city or country of residence;
- broad property interest;
- language;
- consent.

Collect sensitive qualification data only later and only when operationally required.

**Tags**

`low-friction registration`, `multi-step form`, `sticky mobile CTA`, `progressive profiling`, `WhatsApp confirmation`, `consent UX`

### 5.7 Multi-location architecture

**Market pattern**

Cityscape Events and United Property Expo present many event markets within a recognizable parent system.

**Spimar direction**

Use one component system and CMS schema with controlled local variation:

```text
Global platform
├── Event directory
├── Paris event
├── Brussels event
├── Montreal event
├── Düsseldorf event
├── Abu Dhabi event
└── Future markets
```

Shared components:

- header;
- event hero;
- trust metrics;
- benefits;
- exhibitors;
- agenda;
- gallery;
- FAQ;
- registration;
- footer.

Localized data:

- market;
- language;
- date and venue;
- event status;
- imagery;
- copy;
- partners;
- agenda;
- legal consent;
- campaign tracking.

**Tags**

`multi-event platform`, `country landing pages`, `CMS-driven pages`, `localized campaigns`, `event directory`, `content variants`

## 6. Visual direction research

### 6.1 Recommended aesthetic

The visual system should feel:

- architectural rather than template-driven;
- premium but approachable;
- international but recognizably Moroccan;
- dark, layered and warm;
- editorial rather than dashboard-like;
- grounded in real people and real places;
- confident without appearing exclusive or intimidating.

### 6.2 Recommended design ingredients

- deep charcoal-green background;
- warm ivory body text;
- emerald or turquoise primary accent;
- limited warm brass/gold secondary accent;
- strong display typography paired with highly readable body text;
- large architectural crops;
- event photography with human interaction;
- thin structural lines inspired by plans, grids and routes;
- restrained Moroccan geometry used as a system, not decoration;
- layered panels rather than excessive glassmorphism;
- motion that reveals relationships and place.

### 6.3 Photography system

Use four distinct media categories:

1. **Documentary proof:** genuine prior-event photographs
2. **Property opportunity:** approved developer/project photography
3. **Place and belonging:** real Moroccan city and lifestyle imagery
4. **Campaign atmosphere:** clearly promotional Higgsfield imagery or video

Generated media must never be presented as evidence of attendance, partners, venues or completed property projects.

### 6.4 Motion system

Recommended:

- quiet hero image drift;
- mask-based image reveal;
- location transitions;
- metric count-up after entry;
- subtle card depth;
- partner-grid movement only when user-controlled;
- short cinematic campaign loop with mobile fallback;
- reduced-motion support.

Avoid:

- long loaders;
- mandatory scroll-jacking;
- custom cursors;
- excessive WebGL;
- autoplay audio;
- fast decorative animation;
- motion that delays registration.

## 7. Multilingual and RTL design implications

French, Arabic and English must be designed as separate compositional realities, not only translated strings.

Requirements:

- semantic `dir="rtl"` at document or route level;
- logical CSS properties;
- mirrored directional icons where meaning requires it;
- non-mirrored brand marks, photographs and media controls;
- flexible headings that support different line lengths;
- Arabic font selected for premium display and body readability;
- independent copy review for every language;
- localized number, date, telephone and address formatting;
- no text embedded in generated images or videos;
- safe expansion space in buttons, cards and navigation;
- language preference retained across event pages.

The hero composition should be tested in all three languages before approval because headline shape will materially change the visual balance.

## 8. Business and marketing inspiration

### 8.1 Three user journeys

The platform should serve:

1. **Visitor:** discover event → trust value → register → attend
2. **Exhibitor:** understand audience → request participation → qualify
3. **Partner:** understand platform reach → explore collaboration → contact

The homepage can introduce all three, but each event landing page should prioritize visitor registration.

### 8.2 Content flywheel

Every event should generate reusable content:

```text
Campaign launch
→ exhibitor announcements
→ expert/property education
→ registration campaign
→ event coverage
→ testimonials and highlights
→ post-event lead nurturing
→ proof for the next location
```

This changes the website from a temporary landing page into a year-round acquisition system.

### 8.3 Recommended lead ladder

- Low commitment: explore city/event
- Medium commitment: save date or request reminder
- Main conversion: free registration
- High intent: request personalized orientation
- B2B: exhibit or partner inquiry

### 8.4 Campaign content pillars

- Own in Morocco
- Invest with clarity
- Meet trusted actors
- Compare in one place
- Finance your project
- Reconnect with Morocco
- Previous-edition proof
- Event countdown and practical preparation

## 9. Patterns to avoid

- Generic “discover your dream property” language
- A hero that hides date, venue or registration
- Decorative partner logos without context
- Unverified statistics
- Fake scarcity
- Generated people presented as former attendees
- Heavy cinematic effects before useful information
- Too many CTA labels
- Asking for budget and income in the first form
- Separate hard-coded websites for every country
- Embedded text inside campaign media
- Fixed-height layouts that fail in Arabic
- Automatic carousels that hide exhibitors
- Dark interfaces with poor input and body-text contrast
- Country flags as the only language selector
- Property-marketplace complexity on an event page

## 12. Recommended main landing-page direction

The main homepage should not duplicate an individual event page. It should establish Spimar as the parent platform and route users toward a relevant active event.

Proposed sequence:

1. Global brand hero and next-event spotlight
2. Upcoming event locations
3. What Spimar enables
4. Verified platform impact
5. Developers, banks and partners
6. Morocco destination/opportunity editorial section
7. How the visitor journey works
8. Previous editions and real proof
9. Visitor testimonials
10. Exhibitor/partner pathway
11. FAQ
12. Final event-selection CTA

Each event-location page then uses a more direct conversion sequence:

1. Event hero
2. Trust strip
3. Why attend
4. Exhibitors
5. Event experience and agenda
6. Projects/destinations preview
7. Previous-edition proof
8. Registration
9. Venue and practical information
10. FAQ
11. Final registration CTA

## 13. Design hypotheses to test

1. A human/event-led hero will create more trust than a property-only hero.
2. Showing city, date and “free invitation” above the fold will improve registration starts.
3. Recognizable developer and bank logos will outperform generic quality claims.
4. A categorized exhibitor preview will improve both visitor registration and exhibitor sales.
5. Previous-event video and real photographs will reduce perceived risk.
6. A short first-stage form will reduce mobile abandonment.
7. Personalized event routing by country/language will outperform a generic country grid.
8. A restrained dark editorial system will create stronger premium perception than gold-heavy luxury styling.

Recommended measurements:

- event-card click-through rate;
- registration start rate;
- registration completion rate;
- form abandonment by field;
- exhibitor-card engagement;
- video/gallery engagement;
- CTA performance by placement;
- conversion by language and market;
- mobile Core Web Vitals;
- qualified lead rate after attendance.

## 14. Immediate implications for the next phase

The next UX architecture iteration should:

1. Define the homepage/event-page boundary.
2. Create the exact global homepage section structure.
3. Create the reusable location-page structure.
4. Define visitor, exhibitor and partner entry points.
5. Lock the CTA vocabulary in French, Arabic and English.
6. Define the exhibitor taxonomy.
7. Define the event lifecycle states: upcoming, registration open, full, live, completed and archived.
8. Map real available assets to each section.
9. Identify missing Higgsfield campaign assets.
10. Produce low-fidelity desktop and mobile wireframes before visual generation.

## 16. Final research conclusion

Spimar should not attempt to become the most visually extravagant real-estate website. Its opportunity is more valuable:

> Become the clearest, most trusted and most reusable digital bridge between Moroccans abroad and credible property opportunities in Morocco.

The winning product will combine real event proof, modern Moroccan identity, premium architectural storytelling, practical decision support and low-friction registration inside one multilingual, CMS-driven multi-event platform.
