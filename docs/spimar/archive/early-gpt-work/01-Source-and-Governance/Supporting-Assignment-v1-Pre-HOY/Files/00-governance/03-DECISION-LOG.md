# Decision Log

**Status:** `ACTIVE`

## DEC-001 — Official CTO brief is the controlling input

- **Status:** `APPROVED`
- **Date:** 2026-07-29
- **Decision:** The new assignment overrides conflicting visitor-first plans and
  generated concepts.

## DEC-002 — Primary customer is the exhibitor

- **Status:** `APPROVED`
- **Decision:** Moroccan property developers and their executives are the global
  website's primary audience.
- **Consequence:** Exhibitor promise, proof and conversion dominate the parent site.

## DEC-003 — Dual journeys remain explicit

- **Status:** `APPROVED`
- **Decision:** Maintain separate exhibitor and visitor spaces.
- **Consequence:** Forms, CTAs, content and analytics cannot be mixed.

## DEC-004 — Exhibition cards are a priority surface

- **Status:** `APPROVED`
- **Decision:** Country/city editions appear prominently near the top of the homepage.
- **Consequence:** Cards require lifecycle, availability, media and dual-action states.

## DEC-005 — Parent domain plus localized subdomains

- **Status:** `APPROVED`
- **Decision:** `spimarimmo.com` is the global platform; localized event experiences
  remain on approved subdomains.
- **Consequence:** One host-aware application and CMS model serve the network.

## DEC-006 — Multilingual baseline

- **Status:** `APPROVED`
- **Decision:** French, English and Arabic/RTL are first-class initial locales.
- **Consequence:** Design, content, forms, SEO and QA cover all three.

## DEC-007 — Responsive quality across all primary viewports

- **Status:** `APPROVED`
- **Decision:** Mobile-first is an implementation discipline, not a mobile-only product.
- **Consequence:** Desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile receive intentional compositions.

## DEC-008 — WellExpo as reference, not product template

- **Status:** `APPROVED_FOR_EXPLORATION`
- **Decision:** Use its modern editorial event grammar as the main visual reference.
- **Consequence:** Do not copy its assets/code or let its conference IA override the
  official B2B content model.

## DEC-009 — Modern, not luxury

- **Status:** `APPROVED`
- **Decision:** The design must be sophisticated through typography, grid, media,
  detail and motion—not luxury real-estate clichés.

## DEC-010 — Evidence before claims

- **Status:** `APPROVED`
- **Decision:** No metric, case outcome, logo, quote or expected attendance is
  published without source and approval.

## DEC-011 — Authentic media

- **Status:** `APPROVED`
- **Decision:** Real event proof uses real SPIMAR media. Generated imagery may only
  support non-documentary campaign atmosphere.

## DEC-012 — Previous workspace becomes historical

- **Status:** `APPROVED`
- **Decision:** `spimar-workspace/` remains intact but is not the active source of truth.

## DEC-013 — Clean frontend implementation

- **Status:** `APPROVED`
- **Decision:** Start a new Next.js application after upstream gates pass.

## DEC-014 — Existing CMS reuse

- **Status:** `PROPOSED`
- **Decision:** Audit and potentially retain the current headless WordPress/WPGraphQL
  layer behind a content adapter.
- **Approval required from:** CTO/technical owner/content owner.

## DEC-015 — Operational database

- **Status:** `PROPOSED`
- **Decision:** Use Supabase/PostgreSQL for leads, consent, appointments, attendance,
  attribution and integration state.

## DEC-016 — CTA wording

- **Status:** `PROPOSED`
- **Decision:** Global primary CTA `Devenir exposant`; secondary `Télécharger la
  brochure`; qualified pages may use `Réserver un rendez-vous` or `Demander une
  proposition`.
- **Validation:** Commercial team and language review.

## DEC-017 — Design theme rhythm

- **Status:** `PROPOSED`
- **Decision:** Dark-led event scenes with controlled light editorial chapters and
  SPIMAR yellow as action/energy.
- **Validation:** High-fidelity screen review.
