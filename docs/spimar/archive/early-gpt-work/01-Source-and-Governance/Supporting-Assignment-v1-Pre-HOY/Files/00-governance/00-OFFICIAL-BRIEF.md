# Official CTO Brief — Normalized Product Requirements

**Status:** `AUTHORITATIVE_INPUT`  
**Received:** 29 July 2026  
**Scope:** `spimarimmo.com` and its localized event subdomains

## 1. Business objective

The website's main objective is to convince Moroccan property developers to
exhibit at SPIMAR's international exhibitions.

It is not a conventional institutional website and not primarily a visitor
registration landing page. It must work as a B2B sales instrument for commercial
directors, marketing directors and executive leadership.

### Fundamental question

> Pourquoi devrais-je investir plusieurs dizaines de milliers de dirhams pour
> exposer avec SPIMARIMMO ?

Every important page must help answer that question through outcomes, evidence,
process, visibility, market insight and risk reduction.

## 2. Commercial hierarchy

### Primary journey — exhibitors

1. Understand SPIMAR's promise.
2. Identify the relevant international editions.
3. Evaluate visitor quality and market access.
4. Understand the marketing and operational support.
5. Review evidence, cases, testimonials and partners.
6. Compare exhibitor packages.
7. Download the brochure or request a meeting.
8. Enter a qualified commercial follow-up process.

### Secondary journey — visitors

1. Find the relevant exhibition.
2. Review the exhibitors and programme.
3. Understand practical information.
4. Pre-register.

The visitor journey remains important because visitor quality is part of the
product sold to exhibitors.

## 7. Exhibition-card priority

Country and city event cards must be visually prominent. They are not a small
navigation appendix.

Every exhibition record must support:

- country and city;
- lifecycle status;
- date and timezone;
- hotel/venue;
- expected visitors, with evidence status;
- history and edition number;
- real photo and video;
- brochure;
- exhibitor action;
- visitor action;
- localized subdomain.

## 8. MRE market-intelligence topics

Explain, with appropriately sourced evidence:

- main residence;
- secondary residence;
- retirement;
- return to Morocco;
- wealth/property investment;
- family transmission.

These are content hypotheses until validated by research or first-party SPIMAR
data. They must not be presented as quantified truth without sources.

## 9. Conversion definition

### Primary conversions

- exhibitor brochure download;
- exhibitor enquiry;
- qualified meeting booking;
- proposal request;
- stand/package reservation recorded offline or in CRM.

### Secondary conversions

- visitor pre-registration;
- newsletter/resource subscription;
- WhatsApp contact;
- press or speaker request.

## 10. Non-negotiable quality conditions

- B2B message is visible in the first viewport.
- Event locations are prominent and understandable.
- Proof appears before long institutional history.
- The page does not fabricate metrics or commercial outcomes.
- Visitor and exhibitor forms do not share the same undifferentiated flow.
- The experience is excellent on desktop and fully responsive on small devices.
- Local editions inherit one system rather than separate copy-pasted websites.
