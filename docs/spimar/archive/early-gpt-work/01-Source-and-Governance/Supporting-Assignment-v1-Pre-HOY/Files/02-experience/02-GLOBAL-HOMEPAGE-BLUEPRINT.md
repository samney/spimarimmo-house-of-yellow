# Global Homepage UX and Content Blueprint

**Status:** `PROPOSED_FOR_WIREFRAMING`  
**Route:** `spimarimmo.com/[locale]`  
**Primary conversion:** Exhibitor enquiry or meeting  
**Secondary conversion:** Brochure download  
**Tertiary route:** Find a visitor exhibition

## 1. Page strategy

The page must sell a commercial system, not list institutional facts. Its rhythm is:

```text
Promise
→ proof
→ international opportunities
→ objection handling
→ outcome evidence
→ delivery method
→ market insight
→ offer
→ action
```

## 2. Section order

### H01 — Global header

**Purpose:** Establish the two journeys and keep the exhibitor action persistent.

**Content**

- global logo;
- exhibitions;
- why exhibit;
- results;
- offers;
- resources;
- visitor space;
- locale;
- `Devenir exposant`.

**UX**

- transparent over hero, then solid/sticky;
- full keyboard support;
- dark/light treatment follows section contrast;
- mobile menu groups exhibitor and visitor navigation.

### H02 — Hero: commercial promise

**Purpose:** Answer who SPIMAR serves, where and why.

**Content**

- documentary event film or optimized poster;
- official title direction;
- concise supporting copy;
- primary CTA `Devenir exposant`;
- secondary CTA `Télécharger la brochure`;
- one trust cue: years/markets/next edition only when verified.

**Desktop composition**

- wide editorial video plane;
- large but readable display title;
- compact event-network metadata;
- actions separated from the visual noise;
- no visitor form in the hero.

**Mobile composition**

- art-directed poster first;
- title, short copy and actions remain visible without video dependency;
- no mandatory autoplay;
- action targets at least 44 px by product standard.

### H03 — Verified proof bar

**Purpose:** Reduce immediate doubt.

Potential metrics:

- editions organized;
- visitors checked in;
- developers supported;
- countries covered;
- qualified leads delivered;
- satisfaction/renewal.

Every metric is conditional on the evidence register.

### H04 — International exhibitions

**Purpose:** Make country/event opportunities visible early, as requested by the CTO.

**Structure**

- section statement;
- one featured event card;
- active/upcoming city cards;
- status and availability;
- view-all action;
- separate exhibitor and visitor actions.

**Card data**

- city/country;
- date/status;
- venue;
- short market promise;
- previous-edition proof;
- exhibitor sales status;
- localized subdomain.

**Interaction**

- desktop editorial grid, not a generic equal-card wall;
- mobile horizontal snap or stacked featured-first flow;
- no hidden details that exist only on hover.

### H05 — Why exhibit with SPIMAR?

**Purpose:** Handle the four core objections.

Pillars:

1. Qualified audience.
2. International presence.
3. Large integrated campaigns.
4. Complete support.

Each pillar includes:

- outcome headline;
- concise explanation;
- proof or methodology link;
- relevant real image/data visualization;
- contextual CTA.

### H06 — Featured case study

**Purpose:** Move proof closer to the decision.

Content:

- developer and edition;
- starting objective;
- intervention;
- verified results;
- quote/video;
- full-case-study action.

If no approved case exists, do not replace it with fictional content. Use an approved
method/report preview until evidence is ready.

### H07 — The SPIMAR method

**Purpose:** Demonstrate operational control.

Three chapters:

- Before: campaigns, press, landing pages, registrations and qualification.
- During: reception, content, conferences, meetings and commercial support.
- After: lead delivery, reporting, follow-up and performance review.

**UX**

- desktop timeline or three-act editorial sequence;
- mobile vertical steps;
- outcomes and deliverables, not a decorative process diagram.

### H08 — Visibility system

**Purpose:** Translate marketing activity into exhibitor value.

#### Before

- Meta/Google/YouTube;
- video;
- email/SMS;
- press/influencers.

#### During

- capture;
- interviews;
- social publishing;
- live coverage.

#### After

- reports;
- delivered assets;
- leads;
- follow-up support.

Use a campaign-to-result infographic with approved sample deliverables.

### H09 — Why MRE buy

**Purpose:** Demonstrate understanding of the demand market.

Content themes:

- main residence;
- secondary residence;
- return;
- retirement;
- investment/wealth;
- family transmission.

Rules:

- distinguish research evidence from editorial hypotheses;
- cite source, year, definition and scope;
- combine public market context with first-party visitor data;
- do not treat remittance totals as property-demand statistics.

### H10 — Trusted developers and partners

**Purpose:** Social proof and ecosystem depth.

Content:

- approved developer logos;
- banks and financial partners;
- institutions;
- media;
- edition participation details.

**UX**

- large, high-quality logo field;
- optional filters only with approved categories;
- company detail or edition context on interaction;
- no endless low-contrast marquee as the sole representation.

### H11 — Video testimonials

**Purpose:** Let peers answer commercial objections.

Prioritize:

- director general;
- commercial director;
- marketing director;
- partner/bank decision-maker.

Every video includes:

- name, role and company;
- edition;
- transcript;
- captions;
- approved publication rights;
- concise outcome excerpt.

### H12 — Real event gallery

**Purpose:** Prove scale, atmosphere and execution.

Collections:

- stands;
- attendance;
- meetings;
- conferences;
- property presentations;
- interviews;
- networking.

Separate authentic evidence from generated campaign atmosphere.

### H13 — Exhibitor offers

**Purpose:** Make the next commercial step concrete.

Show:

- target profile;
- included outcomes/deliverables;
- availability by edition;
- `Comparer les offres`;

Do not publish unapproved prices or inclusions.

### H14 — Resources

**Purpose:** Convert earlier-stage prospects and enable evaluation.

- exhibitor brochure;
- guide;
- event calendar;
- plans;
- checklist;
- sample report;
- market brief.

Each download has:

- asset version;
- locale;

- expiry/replacement behavior;

### H15 — Exhibitor FAQ

Prioritize:

- reservation process;
- qualification method;
- expected versus historical attendance;
- cost and what influences it;
- lead access;

- support;
- reporting;

### H16 — Insights preview

**Purpose:** Build organic authority.

Categories:

- Moroccan property market;
- diaspora/MRE;
- investment;
- financing and tax;
- trends;
- interviews and case studies.

Show three to six current, useful items. Do not create an empty “blog” shell.

### H17 — About, team and media confidence

**Purpose:** Confirm operator credibility after commercial value is established.

- history and timeline;
- team and responsibilities;
- Clarkom relationship;
- partners;
- press coverage;

### H18 — Final conversion

**Purpose:** Convert with low ambiguity.

Content:

- strong final outcome statement;
- next relevant exhibitions;
- meeting action;
- brochure action;
- exhibitor contact;
- WhatsApp secondary;
- brief privacy assurance.

### H19 — Footer

Complete global navigation, legal, privacy, cookie preferences, locales, countries and
organizer information.

## 3. Above-the-fold acceptance

Within the first viewport, the user must understand:

- SPIMAR serves Moroccan developers internationally;
- the audience is MRE/investor demand;
- the next action is exhibitor-oriented;
- a brochure is available;
- real exhibition evidence exists;
- visitor registration is not the dominant action.

## 4. Content-density rule

The official brief contains many sections. Avoid a compressed page by using:

- clear chapters;
- one dominant idea per visual composition;
- short summary plus deep route;
- progressive disclosure;
- different section silhouettes;
- deliberate negative space;
- evidence adjacent to claims.

Do not solve density by shrinking type or stacking repetitive cards.

## 5. Responsive hierarchy

Content priority remains stable, but composition changes:

| Range | Composition |
|---|---|
| Wide desktop | Layered editorial grids, larger media, side progress where useful |
| Laptop | Reduced overlap, same content authority |
| Tablet | Two-column where readable; touch-safe controls |
| Mobile | Native vertical flow, featured-first events, concise copy and sticky contextual action |

## 6. Homepage analytics

Track:

- hero exhibitor CTA;
- brochure start/complete;
- event-card impression and selection;
- exhibitor versus visitor route selection;
- case-study open;
- package comparison;
- meeting start/complete;
- FAQ topics;
- resource downloads;
- form errors and success;
- locale/country switching.

No personally sensitive form value enters analytics.

## 7. Homepage review gate

Do not enter high-fidelity design until:

- section order is approved;
- event-card priority is approved;
- primary CTA is approved;
- case-study availability is known;
- package structure is supplied;
- the first verified metric set is identified;
- hero media candidates are inventoried.
